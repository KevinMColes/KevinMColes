# Kevin M. Coles

**Author & Fractional CIO | Founder, Coles Technical Group**

I'm Kevin M. Coles, an author and fractional CIO, and the founder of Coles Technical Group in Arizona. I serve as the technology executive for companies that need one but don't need one full time.

Technology can be operating exactly as intended while the decisions shaping it become increasingly consequential. That is where I work.

My memoir, The One Who Would Be Okay, comes out January 12, 2027. [Pre-order on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HPWVGW3B)
